## How to suggest an improvement

1. **Open an issue first** and describe the suggested change, with enough detail to evaluate it. You can propose dimensional changes, fixes, clearer documentation, or other improvements without uploading a modified STL.
2. Wait for feedback before investing time in a substantial redesign.
3. If you want to submit a model modification, use a pull request **solely to propose inclusion in the official CMP Pogo project**, under the limited permission and contribution terms below.
4. Explain what you changed and why. Do not include third-party models unless you have the necessary rights.

The maintainer may review, request revisions, accept, or decline any contribution. Submitting a proposal does not guarantee inclusion.
